Analytical model

The simulator uses a simplified symmetric four-lobe model. The total applied load is divided equally among the four lobes.

T = F / 4

For the rigid cam model, the force relationship is:

T = N tan(α)

Therefore:

N = T / tan(α)

The friction condition is:

μ ≥ tan(α)

The corresponding friction angle is:

φ = arctan(μ)

The active cam surface is modeled with a logarithmic relation:

r(θ) = r₀ e^(θ tan α)

This lets the contact radius grow as the lobe rotates while preserving the constant-angle behavior associated with logarithmic cam geometry.

EDUCATIONAL MODEL ONLY — This simulator does not represent complete real-world climbing protection behavior. Real cams are affected by elastic deformation, unequal lobe loading, surface roughness, rock quality, crack flare, dynamic loading, manufacturing tolerances, stem orientation, material failure, and other effects. Do not use this simulation to determine whether real climbing equipment or a placement is safe.
